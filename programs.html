Programs of the Ministry

Our programs create opportunities for worship, prayer, teaching, evangelism and spiritual transformation.

A gathering that brings worshipers together to honor God through music and worship.

An outreach focus on sharing the Gospel and being witnesses for Christ.

A youth-focused prayer and Word gathering designed to stir spiritual hunger and transformation.

Week of the Youth Cry

A dedicated period of fasting, prayer, teaching and ministry for young people.

A special conference bringing people together for teaching, worship, prayer and spiritual breakthrough.
