Our Journey

The Story of the Ministry

It Began in Nalerigu

God Has Been Faithful

Mag John Music Ministries Global began in Nalerigu with a burden to reach people through Gospel music and bring nations back to the Father. Since the beginning, the ministry has continued by faith, depending on God's grace, provision and direction.

Through Gospel music, worship, prayer, teaching, evangelism and youth-focused programs, the ministry has continued to reach lives and create opportunities for people to encounter God.

One of the testimonies of the journey is that more than 200 people have given their lives to Christ through the ministry's activities.
