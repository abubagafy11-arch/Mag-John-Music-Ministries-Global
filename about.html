WHO WE ARE

A Ministry Built on Faith

Mag John Music Ministry Global began in Nalerigu with a passion for Jesus Christ and a desire to see people encounter God.

Through the years, God has continued to provide for the ministry, open doors and connect us with people whose lives are being touched through the Gospel.

We believe that every person has a purpose and that the Gospel of Jesus Christ can bring hope, restoration and transformation.

OUR VISION

Reaching Lives for Christ

Our Vision

To see lives transformed by Jesus Christ and believers equipped to fulfill their God-given purpose.

Our Mission

To preach Christ, strengthen believers, raise leaders and reach communities through the Gospel.

Our Values

Faith, prayer, love, integrity, service, humility and commitment to the Word of God.

God Has Been Faithful

We look forward with faith to everything God will continue to do through MJMMGLOBAL.
